If you have been searching for a way to find out what really happens to your leads after you hand them off, you have probably run into two names: Assumed and DummyLead. Both are built on the same core idea, planting believable decoy contacts (called [seed leads, decoy leads, or test leads](/blog/decoy-leads-seed-leads-test-leads/)) into a flow you want to watch. From there, the two products point in different directions, and picking the right one comes down to which question keeps you up at night.

This is our honest read on the difference. We build DummyLead, so weigh that however you like, but we would rather you end up on the tool that fits your problem.

## The one-sentence version

**Assumed watches what partners do with your data. DummyLead watches how people handle your leads.**

If your worry is a data set being resold, shared, or remarketed somewhere it should not be, that is the problem Assumed positions itself around. If your worry is whether a buyer, partner, or rep actually picked up the phone, how fast, how many times, and what they said when someone answered, that is the problem DummyLead was built for.

## What Assumed does

Assumed is a data partner monitoring and vetting platform. Its homepage opens with the question "Do you know what your partners do with your data?", and the product is aimed squarely at that: you plant artificial consumer profiles ("Assumed Seeds") into the data you distribute to partners, affiliates, and buyers, then watch for signals that the data was resold, shared, or remarketed outside the agreement.

That is a real and painful problem, especially for companies distributing large volumes of records across a chain of partners. If a list you sold as exclusive starts generating contact from parties who were never supposed to have it, the seed is how you find out. As of this writing, Assumed lists seed pricing in the range of one to nine dollars per seed per month, which makes sense for seeding at data-set scale.

## What DummyLead does

DummyLead is a [secret shopper for your sales leads](/blog/secret-shopper-for-sales-leads/). Each Dummy Lead is a full working identity: a real name, a dedicated inbox, and a live phone number that receives calls and texts. You drop it into a lead flow (a buyer feed, a partner hand-off, or your own call rotation) and it gets treated like any other lead, because nobody handling it can tell it apart.

Because the seed experiences everything a real prospect would, you get the whole picture, not just a resale alarm:

- **Speed to contact.** The measured gap between the lead arriving and the first genuine call, text, or email, timestamped from the prospect's side rather than [self-reported in a CRM](/blog/speed-to-lead-what-fast-follow-up-looks-like/).
- **Follow-up cadence.** Every attempt, on every channel, so you can see whether follow-up is a real sequence or one dial and silence.
- **What was actually said.** Calls are recorded and transcribed, texts and emails are saved word for word, so you can check tone and script adherence, not just activity.
- **Resale, caught in the act.** Each seed is exclusive to one recipient, so a second caller on an "exclusive" lead is proof it was passed on, the same way you would [audit a lead buyer or partner](/blog/how-to-audit-a-lead-buyer-or-partner/) without tipping them off.
- **Caller reputation.** Every inbound call is scored for spam risk and SHAKEN/STIR attestation, so you also learn whether the people calling your leads are burning answer rates.

## Where they overlap, and where they split

The overlap is resale detection. Both products catch an "exclusive" record being contacted by someone who should never have had it, and if that is your only question, either approach can answer it.

The split is depth versus breadth. Assumed's framing is the data chain: many seeds spread across distribution at low cost per seed, watching where records travel. DummyLead's framing is the contact experience: fewer, fully alive seeds that measure how a lead is worked, minute by minute, call by call. One tells you where your data went. The other tells you what it was like to be your lead.

## How to choose

Pick based on the question you need answered:

- **"Is my data being resold or misused somewhere in my distribution chain?"** Start with a data partner monitoring approach like Assumed's.
- **"Are my buyers, partners, or reps actually working the leads I give them?"** That is speed to contact, attempt counts, and recorded calls, which is DummyLead.
- **"I sell exclusive leads and need proof buyers keep them exclusive."** Both can catch the resale; DummyLead also hands you the timestamped timeline and recordings to put in front of the buyer.
- **"My answer rates are collapsing and I do not know why."** You want visibility into spam labeling and attestation on the numbers dialing your leads, which is part of every DummyLead seed.
